## 🎯 Key Improvements for Beginners

### 1. **No Technical Jargon**
- ❌ "ADX", "ATR", "P/E Ratio", "D/E Ratio"
- ✅ "Price Direction", "Price Stability", "Price Tag", "Company Debt"

### 2. **Simple Language**
- ❌ "Volatility category", "Institutional ownership"
- ✅ "Jumpy prices", "Professional investors"

### 3. **Clear Explanations**
- ❌ "Liquid (85/100)"
- ✅ "Super easy! Lots of buyers & sellers"

### 4. **Actionable Insights**
- ❌ "Extreme volatility"
- ✅ "High risk - only for experienced traders"

### 5. **Visual Indicators**
- ✅ Color-coded warnings (Red = danger, Green = good)
- ✅ Clear icons (⚡ = jumpy, 💎 = cheap)

### 6. **Relatable Analogies**
- "Like buying toys on sale" (cheap valuation)
- "Lots of buyers & sellers" (liquidity)
- "Free money" (dividends)
